Abstract Clocks - Tim Holman

I find this exercise in web design to be a very fun way to think about time, referencing time as a numeral concept, but divorcing its presentation from the numbers. Time, here, is represented as a flow of color and gradients.

The Deep Sea - Neal Agarwal

This project ties scrolling on a webpage to diving into the water by exploring the realitive depths of marine life as the viewer travels farther down the page.

Pink Trombone - Neil Thapen

This site creates a comical depiction of the human noise machine and allows the user to digitally play around with the mechanisms of human voice.
